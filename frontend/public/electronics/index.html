ELECTRONICS BENCH / STUDY 001

Make a little light.

A breadboard, a resistor, and one red LED. Follow the path from supply to ground.

01 / Breadboard wiring

5 V DC · 220 Ω · red LED

Magnified breadboard section · A–E holes in the same numbered column share a connection. Columns 16 and 17 are separate.

Power off · 0 mA

02 / Build order

  1. With power off, connect +5 V to E10.
  2. Place the 220 Ω resistor between A10 and A17.
  3. Put the LED’s anode (+) in E17 and cathode (−) in E16. The cathode is usually the short lead beside the flat edge.
  4. Connect D16 to supply ground, then turn on the supply.
ESTIMATED LED CURRENT13.6 mA

(5 V − 2 V) ÷ 220 Ω

Assumes a 2 V LED forward drop. Actual current depends on the LED. The toggle illustrates this circuit; it is not a SPICE simulation.

03 / The electrical path

+5 V→220 Ω resistor→LED anode (+)→LED cathode (−)→GND

The resistor limits current. Keep it in series with the LED.

04 / Component library

Solderless breadboard

Connected terminal groups hold components without solder.

220 Ω resistor

Red–red–brown value bands. A ¼ W part is ample for this circuit.

5 mm red LED

Polarized component: anode to the resistor, cathode to ground.
